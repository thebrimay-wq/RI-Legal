How We Work

Four steps, no surprises

  1. Consultation

    Thirty minutes, confidential, no charge. Bring what you have. We will tell you whether you have a matter worth pursuing.

  2. Written scope and fee

    You get the work, the fee, and the timeline in writing before we start. Flat fee where we can quote one, hourly where we cannot.

  3. The work

    You are copied on every filing and hear from us at each decision point. No update means nothing has changed, and we will say so.

  4. Close-out

    A final letter that says what happened, what you now hold, and what to keep on file. Your documents are yours to take.
