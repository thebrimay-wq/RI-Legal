01 / About the firm

Two practices that keep running into each other.

A client buys a duplex, refinances it, picks up the fourplex next door, then wants all of it to reach their children without a California probate. Property work and estate work land on the same desk for a reason. At RI Legal Group they land on ours.

We take matters throughout California, for homeowners, families, investors, developers, and the businesses signing commercial leases. The firm stays small on purpose: the attorney who takes your call is the one who reads the title report and the one who drafts the trust.

Matters outside real estate and estate law go to attorneys who handle them every week, and we make the introduction.

02 / What we do

Six lanes, one file.

Everything below runs across one desk, so the lease you signed in 2019 is already on file the day you decide to sell the building.

01

Residential Real Estate

Purchase and sale agreements, escrow instructions, title and disclosure review, and deed transfers. Boundary, easement, and nondisclosure disputes, plus the defect that surfaces on the title report a week before escrow closes.

  • Purchase & sale agreements
  • Escrow instructions and title review
  • Disclosure and nondisclosure disputes
  • Deeds, refinances, transfers
02

Commercial Real Estate

Acquisitions and dispositions of retail, office, industrial, and multifamily property. Due diligence, purchase money financing, entity structuring, and 1031 exchanges held to their deadlines.

  • Acquisitions and dispositions
  • Due diligence and survey review
  • Acquisition and refinance lending
  • 1031 exchanges and holding entities
03

Leasing & Landlord Work

Commercial leases from the letter of intent to the estoppel. Build-out allowances, CAM and escalation terms, assignments and subleases, plus the unlawful detainer when a tenant stops paying.

  • Landlord and tenant lease drafting
  • Build-out, CAM, and escalations
  • Assignments, subleases, estoppels
  • Defaults and unlawful detainer
04

Land Use & Development

Zoning relief, variances, conditional use permits, tentative parcel and tract maps, CEQA review, and appearances before city and county planning commissions across the state.

  • Variances and conditional use permits
  • Parcel and tract map approvals
  • Planning commission hearings
  • CEQA and coastal permit coordination
05

Estate Planning

Revocable living trusts, wills, powers of attorney, and advance health care directives. Plans written around the property you already hold, so the deed and the trust say the same thing and the house never reaches probate.

  • Revocable living trusts and wills
  • Powers of attorney and directives
  • Deed and beneficiary alignment
  • Succession for family-held property
06

Probate & Trust Administration

Probate before California Superior Courts, trust funding and administration, sales of estate real property, and clean transfers of title to the beneficiaries.

  • Estate administration and accounting
  • Trust funding and distribution
  • Sales of estate real property
  • Title transfers to beneficiaries

03 / How we work

Four steps, no surprises.

  1. Consultation

    Thirty minutes, no charge. Bring the offer, the deed, the lease, or a list of what you own. You leave knowing what the matter takes.

  2. Scope and fee in writing

    The work, the fee, and the dates go on paper before anything starts. Flat fee where we can quote one, hourly where the matter will not sit still.

  3. The work

    You get copies of every filing and a call at each decision point. Silence from us means nothing moved, and we tell you that too.

  4. Close-out

    A complete document set or a signed estate plan, a letter on what you now hold, and a short list of what to keep.
